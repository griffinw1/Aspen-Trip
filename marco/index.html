Travel day. The group meets in Denver, picks up the Suburban, and drives west. Stop in Glenwood Springs for the week’s groceries. By late afternoon you’re on Red Mountain with the week ahead — and after dinner, four of you are at Belly Up for Cloonee.

First 48 hoursGroceries to grab on the way inOrder ahead for curbside pickup at City Market, Glenwood Springs →

Beer and wine: Colorado grocery stores sell both, but plan to buy them in the store with ID rather than in the pickup order. Spirits need a liquor store. Groceries are far cheaper here than in Aspen.

    Before You Go

    Packing & What to Wear

    Aspen in late December is cold, bright, and bone-dry at altitude. Layering is the whole game — you peel down on a bluebird groomer and pile back on for a windy summit or a 9 AM snowmobile run.

    The Forecast, Roughly
    20–35°F
    Daytime on mountain
    5–15°F
    Summit / wind chill
    10–25°F
    Night in town
    8,000′
    Base elevation
    Dry
    Strong sun · low humidity

    Sun reflecting off snow at altitude burns fast even at 25°F — sunscreen and lip balm are not optional. Glenwood (hot springs day) runs a touch milder than Aspen.

    ⚠Snowmobile day · read this

    T-Lazy-7 provides helmets only. Everyone on the Day 3 tour — including Jim and anyone not skiing — must bring their own warm ski-level outerwear, waterproof gloves, and snow boots. If Jim isn’t renting ski clothing for the week, he still needs a full warm kit for that morning. (Snowsuits are sometimes available at the ranch — call (970) 925-4614 to confirm; don’t count on it.)

    The Layering System

    Build every cold day from this
    • Base layer — merino or synthetic top + bottoms (no cotton)
    • Mid layer — fleece or light puffy for insulation
    • Shell — waterproof/breathable ski jacket + pants
    • Ski socks — tall, merino, one clean pair per ski day
    • Neck gaiter / balaclava & a warm beanie
    • Liner gloves to wear under ski gloves on the cold mornings

    On the Mountain

    Skiers · Griffin, Ben, Marco, Stella, Marcy
    • Goggles (plus a low-light lens for flat days) & sunglasses
    • Waterproof ski gloves or mittens + hand/toe warmers
    • Helmet — rentals include skis/boots, bring or rent a helmet
    • Small backpack for layers, water, snacks, sunscreen
    • Ski pass / phone wallet in a zip pocket
    • Boots & helmet stay with each skier between mountains

    Snowmobile Day (all 6)

    Wed Dec 30 · bring your own
    • Full warm outerwear — jacket + pants/snow bibs
    • Waterproof snow boots (not just après boots)
    • Warm waterproof gloves — mittens are warmest
    • Goggles or sunglasses · neck gaiter · warm hat under helmet
    • Hand warmers — you’re moving fast in cold air
    • Jim included — he needs this kit even though he’s not skiing

    Evenings & Town

    Dinners, après, walking around
    • Warm après / snow boots with grip for icy sidewalks
    • Smart-casual for the marquee dinners (Matsuhisa, Steakhouse 316)
    • A dressier layer for NYE
    • Warm coat, scarf, gloves for walking between spots
    • Cozy indoor layers for cook-in nights at the house

    Hot Springs Day

    Fri Jan 1 · Iron Mountain
    • Swimsuit (wear under clothes to save a changing trip)
    • Sandals / slides for the deck · a warm robe or layer
    • Hair tie · goggles optional · quick-dry layer
    • Towels available on site — or bring your own

    Health & Altitude

    8,000′+ is no joke the first days
    • Sunscreen + SPF lip balm — reapply on the mountain
    • Hydrate hard — altitude + dry air dehydrate you fast
    • Ibuprofen / your meds · electrolyte packets
    • Lotion & saline spray for dry skin and sinuses
    • Go easy on alcohol the first night or two

    Don’t Forget

    The little stuff that derails a trip
    • IDs, cards, insurance card · phone + chargers
    • Photo ID for Belly Up (21+) · Cloonee tickets installed in the Belly Up app
    • Ski passes / confirmations (see the Contacts tab)
    • Battery pack for cold-drained phones on the mountain
    • Reusable water bottle · daypack · small first-aid kit
    • Groceries are handled Day 0 — just bring any specialty items

    No Gear? Rent It

    For Jim or anyone without outerwear
    • Kit Lender — ships full jacket/pant kits to the house (kitlender.com)
    • Suit Yourself — Aspen outerwear specialist, (970) 920-0295
    • Four Mountain Sports — skis, boots, helmets, accessories
